From the owner

Thakur Ji

We started this hotel in 2012 with a handful of rooms and a small kitchen. My wife’s dal was the reason the first customers came back, and my brother handled the rooms himself.

Mauhar gets travellers at every hour, so we keep the reception open all night. If a family arrives at 2 AM after a long journey, somebody is awake, and a hot meal is not a problem.

Come as a guest. We want you to leave like family.

  • Rooms cleaned fresh before every guest
  • Pure vegetarian kitchen

  • Free parking for cars and bikes
  • Clear prices, no hidden charges
